# Proactivity and Interruption Policy

## Principle

Proactivity is valuable only if WBT knows when not to speak.

## Levels

### Quiet
Store/correlate; no visible interruption.

### Queue
Place in Presence/Operations attention queue.

### Notify
Non-modal notification.

### Interrupt
High-priority visible/audio alert.

### Propose
Prepare an analysis/action proposal.

## Interrupt criteria

Normally require:
- high relevance
- high urgency/impact
- sufficient confidence
- low duplication
- time sensitivity

## Never interrupt for

- repeated low-value status
- background consolidation completion
- speculative low-confidence observations
- "engagement" reasons

## Feedback

User actions:
- useful
- too noisy
- not relevant
- wrong urgency

feed evals/proposed attention rules.
They do not silently rewrite policy.
